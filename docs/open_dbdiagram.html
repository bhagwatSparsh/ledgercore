LedgerCore → dbdiagram.io

Copies the DBML schema to your clipboard, then opens dbdiagram.io in a new tab. Paste (Ctrl+V) into the editor there and the diagram builds itself.

  1. Click the button above
  2. On the dbdiagram.io tab that opens, click into the editor pane (left side)
  3. Select all and paste (Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+V) to replace the sample schema
  4. The ER diagram renders on the right automatically
